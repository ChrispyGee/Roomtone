# Code of Conduct

Roomtone is a friendly project for people who like music and good software. We want everyone to feel welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect people's time and privacy: don't post other people's personal details, room names or network information.
- Credit others' work.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Anything that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and in any space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub (for example by opening a *security advisory* draft, which only maintainers can see, or by messaging a maintainer). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
