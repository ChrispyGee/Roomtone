# Changelog

All notable changes to Roomtone. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). While the version is below 1.0, minor versions may change behaviour.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Search by year.** A *Years* row of decade chips and a *Year* box among the Search page's filters (and a `year` option on the assistant's `search` tool) keeps what came out in a year, a decade (`1980s`, `80s`) or a range (`1985-89`). Typing only a year in the search box does the same. Years come from the tags of your own music, the Internet Archive, podcast episodes and the speakers' library when they say; results from sources that don't say (radio, favorites, most streaming services) are hidden while a year is chosen, and a line says how many. An experimental switch in Settings (*Look up missing years*, off by default) looks up the year of albums that come with none from Apple's iTunes Search, by artist and album name, one at a time, and remembers the answers.
- **Two more assistant tools, for backups on the phone apps.** `export_backup` (look-only) gives a backup as text instead of writing a file, and `restore_backup` (in *Saved items*) adds what a backup's text holds, skipping what is already there. 67 tools now (64 by default, 22 look-only).
- **Four more assistant tools, for the phone apps.** `get_volume_limits` (look-only) and `set_volume_limits` (in *Names and sound*) read and change each room's highest volume, quiet hours and *keep down*; `add_speaker` adds a speaker by its address; `keep_in_step` shows (or, with `apply`, does) copying favorites, playlists and alarms between systems, in *Saved items*. 65 tools now (62 by default, 21 look-only).
- **Tools that list things also return their answer as data.** `list_favorites`, `list_playlists`, `list_recent`, `search`, `browse`, `get_queue`, `list_alarms`, `list_scenes` and `get_lyrics` add a `structuredContent` object to the reply (refs, titles, kinds, positions, times and so on) beside the unchanged text. Apps built on the assistant link, such as the phone apps, read that instead of the sentences.
- **A `get_lyrics` assistant tool.** It returns the words of what a room is playing (timed `[mm:ss.cc] words` lines, or plain text), using the same lrclib.net look-up as the Lyrics section, and only when the *Lyrics* switch is on. Look-only, experimental, 61 tools now. It is what lets the phone apps show lyrics too.
- **Keep systems in step (Settings › Backups, with two or more systems).** Adds the favorites, playlists and alarms one system has to the others so they match, one way or both ways, after showing what each system would get. It only adds; nothing is changed or removed. Alarms need rooms with the same names, and favorites from a music service only play where that service is connected. Tried on the demo system only.
- **Stream bitrate in Technical details (experimental, off by default).** Speakers often report only the format of an internet radio stream. A new switch (*Settings › Playback › Stream details*) has Roomtone open the playing stream's own web address once, read the bitrate and sample rate it announces in its reply headers and close it without playing anything. They show as labels and in *Technical details*, marked as coming from the stream; what the speaker reports still wins. A podcast episode gets an average bitrate (shown as ≈128 kbps) worked out from the file's size and the episode's length. Plain `http(s)` streams and episodes only (not playlist files, segmented streams or services like TuneIn), and some streams say nothing. Separately, *Technical details* now explains an adaptive (HLS) stream from a service, such as BBC Radio: it says the speaker doesn't report its bitrate instead of showing only the bare address type. Tried on a real stream from the command line and on the demo system, not yet with a real station on a real speaker.
- **Scenes can shuffle, and you can save what's playing as a scene.** A scene has a *Shuffle* tick (it turns shuffle on before the music starts; left off, shuffle stays as it is), also in the assistants' `save_scene`. A new *Save what's playing as a scene* button on the Scenes page starts a scene from the selected room or group: its rooms and volumes, shuffle, the sleep timer's time left and, when it can tell, the favorite it is playing; you name it and change what you like. Both are tried on the demo system only. These are the two things the Sonos app's presets had that scenes lacked.
- **Ask where the Microsoft Store submission stands.** The *Microsoft Store package* workflow has a new *status* choice that reads the latest submission's state from Partner Center (draft, in certification, published) without building or sending anything, and a real submission now prints its state at the end. Not tried yet.
- **A room's page now says when a software update is available for that speaker.** The *About this speaker* card has a *Software update* line: *Version … is available* when that speaker is behind, *Up to date* otherwise. Opening the page runs the (read-only) update check once if its system hasn't been checked yet.

### Changed
- **A favorite from a music service that isn't set up on the system now says so in plain words.** The speaker answered *code 402* to a restored favorite whose service (BBC Sounds, Spotify…) hadn't been added on that system, and Roomtone showed the bare error. It now says *“Radio 2” comes from BBC Sounds, which isn't set up on this system yet. Add BBC Sounds in the Sonos app, then try again.* Restoring a backup that adds service favorites also says they only play once the service is added on that system. Confirmed on a real S2 system: after BBC Sounds and Spotify were added in the maker's app, the restored favorites played.
- **Stereo pairs tried on real speakers.** Roomtone read a pair made in the maker's app as one room, and its own *Split* and *Make a pair* worked on two real S2 Play:3 speakers (each took about 5 seconds, and the speakers came back with their old room names). An opt-in test (`ROOMTONE_PAIR_TEST=1 cargo test real_stereo_pair_round_trip -- --ignored`) repeats it. Soundbar sets and mismatched models are still untried.

### Known limits
- **Known limit: a speaker that isn't registered may ignore Roomtone's install.** On a real S2 Play:3 that the maker's app listed as *not registered*, Roomtone's install was accepted twice and nothing happened for 25+ minutes; once the maker's app had registered it and started the update, it finished in under two minutes. Until this is understood, if an install doesn't start (the speaker keeps answering and never restarts), register it in the maker's app and update it there; the check and the *Software update* line still say who is behind.

### Fixed
- **A speaker its neighbour doesn't list is no longer missed, and its software update is no longer overlooked.** A system's rooms were read from one speaker's own list, and speakers don't always agree (a speaker added recently can be missing from an older one's list), so which rooms showed depended on which speaker answered first, and *Check for updates* asked only the first room, so it said "up to date" while another speaker was on older software. Roomtone now asks the other speakers too and adds any room one lists that another doesn't, at start, in the background search and when the layout is read again, and *Check for updates* asks every speaker of the system, offering the update when any of them is behind (an S1 system is still never offered S2 software). *Update all speakers* now updates only the speakers that are behind: each is asked for its software right then, the ones already on the offered build are left alone, the button says *Update speakers that need it* when only some are behind, and the confirmation names them. Tried on a real S2 system where one speaker's list was missing the other and one of the two was behind (nothing installed).
- **Restoring a backup now lets you choose which systems to restore onto.** It used to restore only onto the system selected in the sidebar, so a backup made on S1 restored with the S2 system selected reported its alarm rooms as missing. *Restore to* lists each system with its rooms and ticks the ones that match the backup; each ticked system gets its own report. When an alarm's room isn't found, the note lists the rooms that system has.
- **The update check no longer fails with "status code 403" when GitHub limits your network.** GitHub allows each network address only 60 anonymous looks an hour, which a shared office or home connection can use up. Roomtone now asks the project's public release page when that happens (and offers a link to the download page, without the in-app *Download*), and if that fails too it says in plain words that GitHub is limiting checks and it will try again later.

## [0.9.6] — 2026-11-24

Roomtone in the Microsoft Store, a signed and notarised Mac download, and a small player from the Windows notification area.

### Added
- **Roomtone is in the Microsoft Store.** [Get it from the Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9MXNHZ6K9NJQ): one-click install, automatic updates, signed by Microsoft. The Windows installer on GitHub stays unsigned (SignPath Foundation declined), so SmartScreen still warns there.
- **Each release is sent to the Microsoft Store automatically.** Pushing a release tag builds the Store package and submits it to Partner Center for certification through Microsoft's Store command line (the Partner Center credentials are repository secrets; without them it only builds). Set-up and the draft-only option are in `docs/MICROSOFT_STORE.md`.
- **The same small player in the Windows notification area (experimental).** With *Open a small player when the icon is clicked* on, a left click on the icon opens a small window beside it: the room to choose, the cover, the song, a seek bar, previous, play or pause and next, the volume (and mute) and *Open Roomtone*. It closes when you click elsewhere or press Esc; a right click still opens the full menu. It works while Roomtone is hidden in the notification area. Not yet tried on a real Windows PC.
- **Mac builds can be signed and notarised.** The release workflow signs the Mac program and `.dmg` with a Developer ID certificate and has Apple notarise the `.dmg`, when the Apple secrets are set (docs/RELEASING.md). Nothing changes until then.
- **A small player in the Mac menu bar (experimental).** *Settings › Appearance › Open a small player when the icon is clicked* (off by default, shown when the menu bar is on): clicking the icon opens a pop-over with the room to choose, the cover, the song, a seek bar, previous, play or pause and next, and the volume. *Open Roomtone* and *More…* sit at the bottom; *More…* and a right click (or Control-click) on the icon open the full menu as before.

## [0.9.5] — 2026-11-23

A Windows notification-area icon, Roomtone opening on computers with basic graphics, the playing room's name beside the Mac menu-bar icon, and scenes that can set sound and speaker layouts.

### Added
- **Roomtone opens on computers with old graphics, such as virtual machines and Remote Desktop (Windows).** Where OpenGL is too old to open the window, Roomtone now starts itself again drawing with DirectX, including Windows' built-in software drawing, and says so in plain words if neither works. Before, the window just never appeared.
- **A notification-area icon on Windows (experimental).** *Settings › Appearance › Show in the notification area* puts a speaker icon next to the clock, with the same menu as the Mac menu bar: what's playing, play and pause, next, previous, volume, your rooms, *Pause all rooms*, *Open Roomtone* and *Quit Roomtone*. A left click opens the window. *Keep running when the window is closed* (on by default once the icon is on) makes the close button hide Roomtone instead of quitting. Off by default. **Built but not yet tried on a Windows PC**; Linux has no icon yet.
- **Scenes can set home-theatre sound and a speaker layout (experimental).** A scene room's *Sound* section now also covers the sub (on or off, level), the rear speakers (on or off), the surround levels for TV and music, the height level and the lip-sync delay, for rooms that have them. A scene can also remember how the speakers are set up (stereo pairs, a soundbar's sub and rear speakers) and make it when it runs: it only adds what is missing, never splits or removes anything, refuses with a reason if something is in the way, and **asks first** because it stops the music in those rooms and can clear a measured Trueplay tuning. It needs Speaker setup switched on. An assistant or the command line runs the rest of the scene and leaves the layout alone. Tried on the demo system only.
- **The Mac menu-bar icon can show the room that's playing (experimental).** *Settings › Appearance › Show the room name next to the icon* (off by default, shown when the menu bar is on) puts the name of the playing room beside the icon and keeps it current, even with the window hidden; the icon is alone when nothing plays. Long names are cut short. Checked with a unit test and a real run on the demo system, not yet tried by hand.
- **Preparation for signed Windows downloads and the Microsoft Store (nothing signed or submitted yet).** A privacy statement (`PRIVACY.md`) and a code signing policy (`CODE_SIGNING_POLICY.md`), the two pages a SignPath Foundation application asks for; a Microsoft Store package definition and build script (`packaging/windows/msix/`) that a workflow builds to check it is well formed; and two maintainer guides, `docs/SIGNING.md` and `docs/MICROSOFT_STORE.md`, with the steps that need an account in the maintainer's name. Neither route is part of the release yet.

### Fixed
- **The S1 system's badge in the sidebar said "S2" in French, Spanish and Italian.** The badge took its letters from the start of the translated name ("Système S1", "Sistema S1"), so it only worked where the name begins with "S1". It now comes from the system itself.

## [0.9.4] — 2026-11-22

Dragging rooms to group them works again, alarms that fade in, the Mac menu bar, sub and surround sound settings, and an experimental speaker setup for stereo pairs and a soundbar's sub and rear speakers.

### Fixed
- **Dragging a room card onto another to group them did nothing.** The drag never started: the card said it wasn't being pointed at while the mouse button was held down, so the press was never noticed. It now starts a drag as designed (found by simulating the mouse in the real window). Dragging a queue row and dropping song files, which were also untried, work in the same test. A new script, `scripts/check-drag.sh`, repeats all three in the demo system and says whether each worked.

### Added
- **Roomtone in the Mac menu bar (experimental).** *Settings › Appearance › Show in the menu bar* adds a speaker icon at the top of the screen. Its menu shows what's playing in the chosen room, with Play/Pause, Next, Previous, Volume (Louder, Quieter, Mute), the list of rooms to choose from, *Pause all rooms*, *Open Roomtone* and *Quit Roomtone*. It is built each time you open it, so it is always current. *Keep running when the window is closed* (on by default once the menu bar is on) makes the red close button hide Roomtone instead of quitting, so alarms, fades and the assistant link keep working; ⌘Q still quits. Mac only for now, off by default. The maintainer tried it on a Mac and the menu works.
- **Speaker setup (experimental): stereo pairs, and a soundbar's sub and rear speakers.** *Settings › Speakers › Speaker setup*, off until you switch on *Allow changing speaker setup*. It lists your stereo pairs (with *Split*) and each soundbar's sub and rear speakers (with *Remove*), and can make a pair from two identical free rooms, or add a sub or two rear speakers to a soundbar. Every change asks first and says that it can clear a room's Trueplay tuning and stops the music in those rooms, and every change can be undone from the same card. Assistants can look at the layout (`get_speaker_layout`) but not change it. **Not tried on real speakers**: the speakers do offer the actions, but what they do to a real system has been tested on the demo system only. Discrete front speakers, custom layouts and layouts inside scenes are still to do.
- **Alarms can fade in over 1 to 30 minutes (experimental).** The alarm editor has a new *Fade in* choice. The speakers only fade an alarm in over about 15 seconds, so Roomtone does the longer rise: the alarm is kept quiet on the speaker, and when it rings Roomtone raises the volume in a straight line to the volume you set. It steps aside the moment you change the volume yourself, and volume limits still apply. **It only works while Roomtone is running**; if it isn't, the alarm stays quiet, and the editor says so. The Alarms page shows *Fades in over N minutes*, the assistant's `list_alarms` mentions it, and backups keep it. Tested on the demo system and with tests, not yet on a real speaker.
- **A Home theatre card in a room's settings, and *Find this speaker*.** For a room that has a sub bonded to it: sub on/off, sub level (−15 to +15) and *invert the sub's phase*. With a pair of surround speakers bonded: surround on/off and the TV and music levels. For an Arc: height speakers level. For a soundbar: lip-sync delay. For a room with a measured Trueplay tuning: switch it on or off (Roomtone can't measure one). The assistant link's `get_sound` and `set_sound` have the same settings (only the ones a room has), and a new tool, `identify_speaker` (59 tools now), and the room page's *Find this speaker* button blink the speaker's status light a few times and put it back. What a room has bonded is read from the speaker's zone group state, because the speakers answer many of these questions with \"0\" even when there is nothing to tune. **New and untried on real home-theatre hardware** (read-only on a real Connect:Amp and Play:3: no controls appear for them, as intended); the ranges are the maker's app's, which the speakers don't publish.

### Changed
- **The roadmap now lists speaker layout setup, layouts in scenes, more room sound settings and a per-speaker EQ as ideas** (stereo pairs, home-theatre sets, front speakers on a soundbar, custom left/right layouts, sub and surround levels, lip-sync, identify a speaker, a graphic EQ), after looking at what a similar independent speaker-setup app offers. Nothing has been built; each needs research and spare speakers first.

## [0.9.3] — 2026-11-22

Volume limits and quiet hours, gentler alarms (copies, several rooms, one-off timers), drag and drop, spoken slider values and a High contrast setting, a now-playing display for picture frames and tablets, a new `get_cover_art` assistant tool, an experimental Stream Deck plugin (in `streamdeck/`, not part of the download), Roomtone looking for a new version when it starts, and a volume step setting. The newest of these (the alarm additions, the now-playing display, drag and drop, volume limits and the accessibility changes) have only been tried on the demo system and with tests, not on real speakers; see each entry.

### Added
- **An experimental Stream Deck plugin** (`streamdeck/`). Buttons and a dial for Roomtone on an Elgato Stream Deck: play/pause, next and previous, now playing, volume up and down (hold to keep going), mute, scenes, pause all, and a volume dial for the Stream Deck +. It talks to Roomtone's assistant link, so Roomtone itself didn't change. Build it with `npm` (see `streamdeck/README.md`) and double-click the file it makes; it isn't released, signed or on the Elgato Marketplace. Tested with unit tests, Elgato's validator, a build and a run against a demo Roomtone, and tried by the maintainer on a real Stream Deck with real speakers (keys and settings page only; **the Stream Deck + dial and touch strip are untried**). A new workflow, `streamdeck.yml`, builds and tests it when it changes.
- **A new assistant tool, `get_cover_art`**, that returns the cover of what a room is playing as a small JPEG picture (it says so if there is none). It is read-only, so it is there in look-only mode too (the assistant link now has 58 tools). The Stream Deck plugin's Now Playing key uses it to show the cover. Checked with a pretend speaker serving a picture; **not yet with a real speaker's cover.**
- **A now-playing display for picture frames, tablets and scripts (experimental).** *Settings › Playback › Now-playing display* (off by default) gives other screens on your network a web address, `http://<this computer>:8791/<secret>/`, that shows the cover and song that's playing, full screen on black. The same address ending in `art.jpg` is just the cover as a JPEG (scaled to `?size=`), and ending in `now.json` describes what's playing. It is read-only, every address starts with a secret (*New address* replaces it), you can pick the room it follows, and it works in `--headless` too. New file `frame.rs` and a new listening port (8791, listed in SPEC §9). Tried with a pretend speaker serving a cover and the demo system in tests, and the page in a real browser; **not yet on a real picture frame or with a real speaker's cover.**
- **Accessibility: sliders say their value in words, a keyboard shortcut picks a room, and there is a High contrast setting.** A screen reader now reads "Volume, 40 of 100" or "Playback position, 1:15 of 3:18" (it used to read a number between 0 and 1); bass and treble say "+2 on a scale from -10 to +10". Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) with 1 to 9 picks the first to ninth room card and says which room it picked. *Settings › Appearance › High contrast* (off by default) uses stronger text, outlines on every control and brighter accent and status colours in both light and dark; tests check the contrast ratios of all four palettes (text 7:1 or better in High contrast, and 4.5:1 in the usual ones; some faint notes and the light-mode orange are only about 3:1 in the usual palettes). Checked with automated tests and pictures; **not tried with a screen reader on Windows or Linux**, and VoiceOver on macOS was not re-run for these changes.
- **Volume limits.** *Settings › Playback › Volume limits*: a highest volume for each room, and quiet hours (a time window, such as 22:00 to 07:00, when every room is held to a lower volume, by the speakers' clock). The limits apply to everything Roomtone sends: the sliders and keyboard, the command line, assistants (which are told when a limit lowered their request), scenes, timers and new alarms. A group stops where its loudest room reaches its limit, and a room already over its limit is never raised further. The speakers have no limit a controller can set (checked on a real S1 room and a real S2 speaker), so another app or the buttons on a speaker can still go higher; an optional switch, *Turn a room back down if it goes over*, makes Roomtone lower such a room every few seconds while it is open. Checked with tests and the demo system; **not yet tried on real speakers.**
- **Gentler alarms: duplicate, several rooms at once, and one-off timers** (all Experimental, like the Alarms page). *Duplicate* in the alarm editor makes a copy to change and save as a new alarm. A new alarm has a *Rooms* checklist and makes one alarm in each room ticked. A *Timer* card at the top of the Alarms page rings a chime in a room after 1 to 720 minutes (quick buttons for 5, 10, 15, 30 and 60): it is a one-time alarm on the speaker, counted from the speaker's own clock, so it rings even if Roomtone is closed, but only to the nearest minute; running timers are listed with *Cancel timer*, and Roomtone deletes a timer's alarm a few minutes after it was due. Checked with tests and the demo system; **not yet on real speakers** (the speakers' clock format was read from a real S1 and S2 speaker, read-only; nothing was made or rung).
- **Drag and drop.** Drag a room card onto another to join them (the dragged room's group joins the one it lands on, which keeps playing). Drag a song in the queue to move it (a line shows where it will land). Drop audio files or folders from your computer on the window to play them in the room you're using (they go in after the song that's playing and start); the window dims and says where they will play. Dropped songs are served to the speakers like songs in your music folders, and nothing else is. Checked by tests: the file part (a dropped song is fetched back the way a speaker would, and survives a rescan) and which rooms a drop joins. **The mouse dragging itself was not tried in a real window or on real speakers, nor was a real drag from the file manager.**
- **A volume step setting, and a button that matches every room's volume.** *Settings › Playback › Volume keys move the volume by* offers 1, 2, 5 (as before) or 10 for Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) with the up and down arrows. On Now Playing, when the rooms in a group are at different volumes, *Match all rooms to this volume* under *All rooms* sets each room to the group volume shown. Both are checked by tests; neither has been tried on real speakers yet.
- **A newer Roomtone can be downloaded from the New versions card.** When a newer version is found, *Download* fetches the installer for this computer (the .dmg on an Apple-silicon Mac, the setup program on 64-bit Windows, the .deb, .rpm or .tar.gz on 64-bit Linux) from this project's own release page into your Downloads folder, checks it against the release's `SHA256SUMS`, and then offers *Open* and *Show in folder*. A file that doesn't match is deleted. Nothing is downloaded until you press the button, and nothing is installed for you. Homebrew installs keep the `brew upgrade` line instead. A new library, `sha2`, does the checking. Checked against the real 0.9.2 release (2026-11-20): the Apple-silicon .dmg (7,699,879 bytes, the size the release lists) downloaded, matched its checksum and left no half-finished file. A file that doesn't match is covered by the code but wasn't tried on a real download.

### Changed
- **The roadmap records that the first step of now-playing art for picture frames has shipped** (experimental, untried on a real frame), and what is left.
- **The roadmap now lists two ideas for other screens and controls, designed together with the web remote:** now-playing art for picture frames, tablets and LED panels (a fixed image address first, then a full-screen page), and an Elgato Stream Deck plugin (ready-made actions, live state and a room picker, instead of hand-made command-line keys).
- **The README now presents the plain .tar.gz and .zip downloads as an advanced option.** The installers are the main choice; the archives (the bare program, handy for the command line, a computer with no screen or a portable copy, and Linux systems without .deb or .rpm) are still attached to every release.
- **The README now shows the one-line Terminal command for opening the unsigned Mac app** (`xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Roomtone.app`), with a link to the full steps. The roadmap also notes creating and splitting stereo pairs as an idea.
- **Roomtone looks for a new version when it starts, not only once a day.** With *Tell me when a new version of Roomtone is out* on, each start looks once a few seconds in (a failed look still backs off), then the daily look carries on. Before, a restart waited out the rest of the day and showed nothing about a newer version it already knew of.
- **A new version found on a Homebrew install shows the command to update it.** *Settings › About › New versions* now says `brew upgrade --cask roomtone` with a *Copy* button when Roomtone was installed with Homebrew; *Open download page* stays as the other choice. Checked on this Homebrew-installed Mac with a pretend newer version (2026-11-20): the command and *Copy* show on their own line under the version line.

### Fixed
- **A failed look for a new version is no longer repeated every 20 seconds.** Offline, or rate limited by GitHub, Roomtone now waits 5 minutes, then 15, 45, about 2¼ hours and at most 6 hours between tries. *Check now* still looks at once.
- **Wired or wireless is now shown for a speaker on Wi-Fi whose traffic isn't counted on its own interfaces.** The check read the speaker's network counters only; some S2 speakers count Wi-Fi traffic on an internal bridge, so the Rooms panel and the assistant's room list showed nothing for them. When the counters can't tell, Roomtone now uses the wireless mode the system reports for that speaker. Checked on a real S2 Play:3 moved between Wi-Fi and cable (2026-11-19): on Wi-Fi the counters were silent and the system reported wireless; on the cable the counters said wired and the system agreed.
- **A speaker that was reset or set up again is believed when it names a different system.** Roomtone used to keep the system it remembered for a known speaker, so a factory-reset speaker stayed under its old system (or showed the old state after being set up again) until the saved speakers were cleared. Now each check of a known speaker asks it which system it belongs to; if it joined a system already listed, the two entries merge. Checked on a real speaker: a forged old system for the S2 Den was replaced with its real one on the next start.

## [0.9.2] — 2026-11-19

A ringing alarm can be snoozed, play along keeps both systems in step, and alarms say when a system has no time zone.

### Changed
- **Alarms are tagged *Experimental*.** The Alarms page, its switch in Settings and the assistant's alarm tools now say so: alarms have had little testing on real speakers, so check that one rings before you rely on it.

### Fixed
- **Linking a room to play along now gives both rooms the same repeat setting and turns shuffle off.** Before, a leader on shuffle and a follower in normal order skipped to different songs on the first *Next* (two systems can't shuffle the same way), and the rooms drifted apart. A notice says when shuffle was turned off. Checked on a real S1 room on shuffle and repeat and a real S2 room in normal order: after linking both reported plain repeat-all. Also checked on those two rooms with a 14-song album (2026-11-19): *Next* on the last song gave no error, and previous, next and removing a song from either room reached the other; the two were only apart because of the shuffle this change fixes.
- **The *Snooze* strip now appears for a ringing alarm in any room.** It used to be refreshed only for the room selected at the bottom of the window (and for other rooms only with *Instant updates from the speakers* on), so an alarm in another room rang with no strip. Every check of a playing room now asks whether an alarm is ringing: one extra request every 4 seconds per playing room. Checked on a real S1 room with no room selected and instant updates off: the speaker's alarm started at 15:35:02 and Roomtone showed it ringing from the next check, 5 seconds later (before, it never did). **Also checked in the real window** (2026-11-19): with another room selected, the strip appeared when the S1 room's alarm started, and *Snooze 5 min* on it silenced the alarm at once (a radio alarm shows as stopped, not paused, while snoozed).
- **Play along keeps both systems in step.** Using either group now moves the other (before, only the leading group was passed on), and removing, moving and clearing songs in the queue is passed on too, so both keep the same queue. If the two end up on different songs, or one plays while the other doesn't, for 12 seconds, the song and play state of the group you used last are copied onto the other (waiting 10 seconds after you last used either group, and at most once every 30 seconds); queues aren't copied again, and if they differ in length you're told to link the rooms again. Checked on a real S1 room and a real S2 room (see below). **Known limit: linking copies the queue, and songs one system can't take are left out** (a 76-song Spotify queue from an S1 room became 74 on the S2 room), so the two queues can differ by a few songs and a removal by position then hits different songs.
- **Next and Previous no longer report an error when there is nothing to skip to.** With *Play along*, the two systems can be on different songs, so one speaker skipped and the other refused with "Next was refused (HTTP 500, code 711)". A speaker with no next (or previous) song is now simply left alone. Other refusals still show.

Roomtone now listens for that (UDP port 1900, shared with other programs; if the port can't be shared, nothing changes) and, when a speaker it isn't talking to announces itself, searches after 3 seconds instead of waiting for the next regular search (every couple of minutes). Never more than one such search every 10 seconds. Speakers going away are still noticed by the regular checks.
- **A crash log that stays on your computer.** If Roomtone closes unexpectedly it now writes what happened to `crash.log` in the settings folder (version, system, time, where, and a backtrace, with network addresses and your home folder blanked out). Settings › About shows a *Crash log* row with the date while the file exists, and the bug report form asks you to attach it. Nothing is sent anywhere.

### Fixed
- **Searching for speakers uses every network connection.** The search used to go out of the computer's default connection only, so a speaker reachable over another one (cable while Wi-Fi is the default, a second adapter, a VPN that takes over the default route) could be missed. The same search is now sent out of each connection that is up (and the default one, as before), up to 16. It adds two small libraries, `if-addrs` and `socket2`. Checked on a Mac with one connection: the same two systems are found as before; a computer with several connections hasn't been tried.
- **Software updates no longer show a raw error for a system that isn't on this network.** A system remembered from another network used to appear under Settings › Speakers › Software updates with a message such as *Couldn't check: … connection timed out*. It now says **Not set up yet. Set this speaker up in the Sonos app, then check again.**, has a *Not set up yet* tag in Settings › Speakers, and has no *Check for updates* button. Found on a new Play:3 whose own update service refuses the check until it is set up (read-only probe, 2026-11-18).

### Security
- **The music server is harder to knock over.** The server that lets speakers fetch music from this computer (port 52880) now answers only computers on a local network, drops requests that aren't sent within 10 seconds or that are oversized (a request line over 8 KiB, more than 64 header lines), drops a speaker that stops reading for two minutes, and serves at most 64 connections at once. Before, anyone on the same Wi-Fi could stall it or make it use up memory by opening slow or endless connections. It never served files outside the folders you added.

### Changed

A single playlist can be exported from its menu on the Playlists page (*Export playlist…*; the file type is chosen in the Save dialog) and imported with *Import playlist*; **M3U/M3U8** is supported too (and *Import playlist* opens `.m3u`/`.m3u8` files, matching file paths to your music folders); and **CSV** (*Import playlist* reads `.csv` lists, also from other services, matching songs without a usable address to your music folders by title and artist). Saving was checked against a real S1 system (read only); restoring to real speakers isn't tried yet.

## [0.5.3] — 2026-11-13

### Fixed
- A settings file that can't be read (a typo from editing by hand, a half-written file) is no longer silently replaced by the defaults: it is moved to `config.json.broken-<n>`, a notice at the top of Settings says where it went, and if it can't be moved nothing is saved that run. Background readers never touch it.

### Added
- **Speaker updates** (Settings › Speaker updates, off by default): instead of asking the speakers again and again, Roomtone subscribes to their UPnP events and re-reads a group the moment its speaker reports a change, so changes made elsewhere show up at once. While events work, polling slows to a safety net (10 s / 30 s / 60 s instead of 1 s / 4 s / 20 s), which also cuts network traffic and CPU use. If the speakers can't reach this computer (a firewall), Roomtone says so and keeps polling as before. Needs an incoming connection from the speakers; only subscribed speakers' notices are accepted. Tried once with a real S1 speaker; not yet with S2. New `speaker_events` field in `config.json`.
- **Settings guide** ([docs/SETTINGS.md](docs/SETTINGS.md)): every setting, where it is in the app, its `config.json` name and default, command-line options and environment variables, and how to back up, move or reset. Linked from Settings › About (*Settings guide*), `--help` and the README.
